# Kickoff Call Playbook — IRS of Las Vegas

**Client:** Independent Restoration Services of Las Vegas
**Channel:** warm intro via rep
**Offer state:** they said yes to the free-upfront-design terms
**Call goal:** capture 10 intake items + set expectations + book the design-review call
**Duration:** 30-45 minutes. Do not run past 45.

---

## Pre-call prep (do this 10 minutes before)

1. **Have a doc open** to type into as they talk. Do NOT try to memorize their answers.
2. **Pull up their national site** (`irs-247.com`) on one monitor and their Vegas IG (`@irsoflv`) on the other. You should be able to reference visible details.
3. **Have your calendar open** so you can book the design-review call at the end.
4. **Have the payment link / invoice tool ready** — even though nothing gets paid on this call, they may ask about it.
5. **Water. Bathroom. Deep breath.** This is your first storm-restoration client. Calm energy sells.

## Opener (2-3 minutes)

Don't over-thank them. It signals you're nervous.

> "Hey [Owner Name], thanks for jumping on. [Rep First Name] speaks highly of you. I'll keep this to 30 minutes — I've got 10 quick questions about the Vegas branch, then I'll show you what I'd build and we'll book the design-review call. Sound good?"

Wait for a nod / verbal yes. Then move.

**Do NOT open by pitching.** They already said yes. This call is scoping, not selling.

## Section 1 — Their Vegas branch (5 minutes, 4 questions)

Ask these in this order. Take notes.

1. **"What's the official name of the Vegas branch — is it Independent Restoration Services of Las Vegas, or do you go by a shorter local name?"**
   - Purpose: `businessName` intake field. Some franchise branches use `[National Brand] of [City]` officially but a shorter local trading name day-to-day. You want both.

2. **"What phone number does a Vegas homeowner call when they've got a burst pipe at 2 AM?"**
   - Purpose: `phone` intake field. Confirm it's a Vegas local number, not the national HQ line.

3. **"Who's the best email for me to send designs, invoices, and anything else project-related?"**
   - Purpose: `email` intake field. Confirm it's the decision-maker, not admin.

4. **"Do you have a domain in mind for the Vegas site, or should we spin up a new one?"**
   - If they have one: get it, verify it resolves. If not: recommend `irsvegas.com` and offer to register it for them (this is a $12/yr expense you cover as part of the $4,800 package — signals professionalism).

## Section 2 — What Vegas homeowners call them about (5 minutes, 3 questions)

This is the copy-mining part. Their answers become the actual website copy.

5. **"Walk me through a typical Vegas emergency call. What time of day, what's usually the damage, what does the homeowner say when they pick up the phone?"**
   - Purpose: hero-line customer language, service order priorities. Take notes verbatim. Their words > yours.

6. **"When Vegas homeowners come to you scared, what's their #1 fear? Not just water damage — what specifically are they afraid of?"**
   - Common answers: mold in the walls, insurance not covering it, having to move out, kids getting sick from chemicals. Their answer drives the FAQ + trust section on the site.

7. **"How fast can you actually get a crew on-site? Give me a real number, not a marketing number."**
   - The answer becomes the hero promise. If they say "usually 45 minutes", the site promises "60-minute response, guaranteed" (always under-promise on the site vs their actual capability).

## Section 3 — Trust + credentials (5 minutes, 3 questions)

8. **"IICRC certifications — do you or your techs hold WRT (Water Restoration Technician), ASD (Applied Structural Drying), and/or AMRT (mold)? Which ones?"**
   - Purpose: trust badge population. If they say "all three", huge trust win. If just WRT, still good.

9. **"Which insurance carriers do you actually work with in Vegas? Not the national list — which ones you've filed claims through in the last 90 days."**
   - Purpose: insurance-carrier logo bar. If they name State Farm, Farmers, USAA, that's your logo lineup. Real relationships > marketing claims.

10. **"Do you have a testimonial from a Vegas homeowner mentioning fast response or an insurance win? Someone I can quote by first name and city?"**
    - Purpose: reviews section + hero social proof. Even one real Vegas testimonial beats 30 stock testimonials.

## Section 4 — Bonus items (3 minutes, 2 questions)

11. **"Do you have a 24/7 answering service for after-hours emergency calls? Someone actually answers, or does it go to voicemail?"**
    - If NO: this is a $500-1000/month add-on Alan sells as part of the "Emergency Machine" tier. If YES: note who provides it (existing service = leave alone).

12. **"Owner photo — do you have a headshot? Are you comfortable being the face of the Vegas branch on the site, or would you rather feature the crew?"**
    - Owner-forward beats anonymous. But if they're camera-shy, crew group photo works. Get their comfort level.

## The show-and-tell (10-15 minutes)

Now you deliver on the offer. Screen-share their national `irs-247.com` and walk through what you'd change for Vegas specifically.

**Use their language back at them.** If they said "our crew is fast" in question 7, echo it: "Your team is fast. But your homepage doesn't say that. Here's what I'd do..."

Show 4 things (max):

1. **The current gap:** open `irs-247.com` and point to (a) no response-time number in the hero, (b) no visible IICRC, (c) no insurance carrier logos as a trust bar, (d) national-only, no Vegas differentiation
2. **What you'd build instead:** describe the fresh Vegas site
   - Hero: "Water damage in Las Vegas? Crew on-site within 60 minutes."
   - Trust bar: IICRC (specific certs they named) + insurance carrier logos (specific ones they named) + BBB
   - 3-step: Call any hour → Crew on-site in 60 min → Dried and documented in N days
   - Text-a-photo secondary CTA
   - Vegas-specific SEO (monsoon-season blog, Henderson/Summerlin/Green Valley/Nellis service-area pages)
3. **Timeline commitment:** design mockup in 5 business days, live site in 21 days from deposit
4. **The offer, restated:** free design upfront. If they love the mockup, $1,000 on approval. $3,800 balance across 60 days. That's it. No hidden fees.

## Handling common questions

**"How do you compare to Servpro?"** — "Servpro is a franchise with call-center dispatch. The Vegas homeowner ends up on hold. Your Vegas branch answers direct. Your site should make that the difference."

**"Do we own the site?"** — "Yes. Full ownership. Delivered to your hosting or I set up hosting for you at cost."

**"What if we don't like the mockup?"** — "You keep the design file and I never bother you again. That's the whole point of the free upfront."

**"What about SEO?"** — "SEO's baked in at Stage 3 of my build process — Google Business Profile overhaul, Vegas-neighborhood pages, schema markup. Not an add-on, part of the $4,800."

**"What about ads?"** — "That's the retainer add-on — $1,500/month for Google LSA + Meta ads if you want it. Optional. Cancel anytime. We can decide after you see the site live and get inbound organic first."

**"How do we pay?"** — "$1,000 on design approval, $3,800 across 60 days — two Net-30 payments of $1,900 each. I can invoice you or send a Stripe link, whichever you prefer."

**"Can we start next week?"** — "The clock starts today. Design mockup lands in your inbox on [Date + 5 business days]. Book me for a design-review call on [Date + 6-7 business days]."

## The close (5 minutes)

Book the next call ON THIS CALL. Do not leave it to email.

> "Alright. I've got everything I need to start. I'll have your design mockup ready by [specific date, 5 business days out]. Let's put a 30-min design-review call on the calendar for [date, 6-7 days out] — that's where you look at the mockup, we adjust anything, and if you approve I send the invoice for the $1,000. Sound good?"

Open your calendar. Send them a calendar invite before you hang up. Do NOT let them leave the call without the next meeting on the calendar.

## Post-call actions (do within 2 hours)

1. **Send a follow-up email** with a summary of what they said, restated in your words, plus the 10 items you captured
2. **Update the brief** — swap `[TO-CAPTURE-AT-KICKOFF]` values for real ones in `research/output/website-factory-brief.md`
3. **Run `/tailor-factory`** — apply the mitigation overrides to the factory chrome
4. **Run `/run-factory`** — kick off Stage 1 (intake validation) with the real client data
5. **Register the domain** if they picked one (do it same day so it doesn't slip)

## Things to NOT do on this call

- **Do not pitch the retainer** as if it's part of the base. Mention only when they ask about ads.
- **Do not go into design details** (colors, fonts, layout choices) — you'll show them the mockup in 5 days. That's the whole design conversation.
- **Do not agree to anything not in the offer.** If they ask for something extra (e.g., email marketing setup, social media management), say: "That's not in the base package. Let's get the site live first, then we can talk about layering that on."
- **Do not apologize for anything.** No "sorry I'm new to storm restoration marketing" or "sorry I don't have case studies yet." They said yes because of the offer's risk reversal, not your case studies.
- **Do not answer questions you don't know.** If they ask something technical you're unsure about, say: "Good question — let me confirm and email you the answer today." Better than guessing.
