# Water/Storm Mitigation — Copy Patterns

## Top 10 hero headlines

1. **Emergency-first:** "24/7 water damage emergency response. Crew on-site within 60 minutes."
2. **Insurance-friendly:** "We bill your insurance directly. You pay only your deductible."
3. **Local specificity:** "{City}'s emergency water damage crew. Available now."
4. **Confidence framing:** "From flood to fully dry in {N} days. IICRC certified."
5. **Anti-mold:** "Water damage in {N} hours. Mold in 24. Call before the clock runs out."
6. **Owner-answers-phone:** "Talk to the owner in under 60 seconds. 24/7 emergency line."
7. **Insurance battle proof:** "We won {N}% of denied claims last year."
8. **Speed-obsessed:** "Fastest response in {city}. On-site or you don't pay."
9. **Text-accessible:** "Text a photo. Get a call in 15 minutes."
10. **Trust-first:** "Certified. Insured. Answering calls at 2 AM since {year}."

## Top 10 CTAs

1. "Call now — 24/7 emergency"
2. "Text a photo of the damage"
3. "Get a crew on-site tonight"
4. "Check if you need mitigation" (curiosity)
5. "Free damage assessment"
6. "Speak with a claims specialist"
7. "See our IICRC certifications"
8. "Get your insurance claim started"
9. "Book an emergency inspection"
10. "Talk to the owner directly"

## Top 10 value props

1. **60-minute response** — hard number, testable, differentiating
2. **IICRC certified** — table-stakes but a signal for uncertified competitors
3. **Direct insurance billing** — the deductible-only offer
4. **All-in-one crew** — water extraction, drying, mold prevention in one visit
5. **Owner-owned, not franchise** — trust vs Servpro/Belfor perception
6. **Text-anytime access** — accessibility during a crisis
7. **Full documentation for insurance** — photos, moisture readings, everything the adjuster needs
8. **No-charge assessment** — reduces friction
9. **Local (not national)** — trust from being nearby, not from a call center
10. **Guaranteed dry** — "guaranteed dry in {N} days or we come back at no charge"

## Common ad hook structures

1. **Water flooding B-roll + urgency voiceover** — "It's midnight. Your basement is flooded. What now?"
2. **Owner-selfie on job site** — "I'm at a job right now. Look what water damage does in 4 hours."
3. **Insurance win testimonial** — "State Farm denied our claim. This company got us $28,000 for a full remediation."
4. **Before/after mold** — thick mold on drywall → clean, sealed, painted
5. **Educational hook** — "If you find this on your baseboard, call within 24 hours" (moisture buckling image)
