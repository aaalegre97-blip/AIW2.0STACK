# Water/Storm Mitigation — Existing Niche-Serving Agencies

## Known agencies in the restoration/mitigation vertical (US)

### Restoration Marketing (restoration-marketing.com), aka RMI
- **Approach:** Niche-specific PPC, GBP, and lead-gen for water/fire/mold restoration franchises and independents. Big on Google LSA (Local Services Ads).
- **Pricing:** $2k-$6k/mo. Lead-gen focus.
- **Sells:** Emergency-call volume. "We'll make your phone ring at 2 AM when someone has a burst pipe."
- **What they miss:** Retention/reputation post-job (customers who churn back to insurance-recommended restoration companies)

### Mold Marketing Pros (moldmarketingpros.com)
- **Approach:** Mold-remediation specific. Meta ads + landing pages heavy.
- **Pricing:** Similar to roofing marketing pros.
- **Sells:** Volume of mold leads.
- **What they miss:** Storm-water damage segment (fire + mold heavy, water damage lighter).

### The Restoration Association / Restoration Sales Success / Restoration Rebels
- Coaching-flavored, not pure agency. Community + services blend.
- Serve owners of restoration companies with content and mastermind offers.
- Weaker on "here's your new website tomorrow" side.

### Blue Corona / High Level HVAC agencies branching into restoration
- Not niche-specific. Sell across all home services. Fine but generic.

## Top 3 patterns winners share
1. **Emergency framing** — "24/7 emergency water damage response" is the money-line. Winners lead with it.
2. **IICRC certification prominently displayed** — the industry-standard credential. Instant trust vs uncertified operators.
3. **Direct-with-insurance language** — "We bill your insurance directly. You pay only your deductible." This is the parallel to storm-restoration roofing's public-adjuster angle.

## Top 3 patterns losers share
1. **Overloaded service menu** — water + fire + mold + biohazard + carpet cleaning + duct cleaning + junk removal. Dilutes the emergency positioning.
2. **No emergency number displayed above fold** — mitigation is a 2 AM call-me-now business. Buried phones lose deals.
3. **No proof of speed** — "how fast will you get here?" is the customer's #1 question. Winners answer it explicitly ("crew on-site within 60 minutes"). Losers don't.
