# Water/Storm Mitigation — CRO Patterns

## Section order (winning restoration/mitigation sites)

1. **Hero with phone-massive** — the phone number is the single largest UI element. Emergency positioning.
2. **60-minute-response commitment strip** — a bold, testable claim right below hero.
3. **Services (compact list)** — Water damage, Fire damage, Mold, Sewage, Storm. 4-6 max.
4. **Emergency process (3-step visual)** — Call → Crew dispatched → Dry within N days.
5. **IICRC + insurance-carrier trust bar** — logos of certifications and major insurance carriers.
6. **Reviews strip** — Google star aggregate + 4-6 testimonial snippets.
7. **Photo gallery / before-after** — water extraction, drying equipment on-site, mold removal.
8. **About** — owner photo + "we answer the phone at 2 AM" positioning.
9. **Service area map** — cities served, response-time estimate by ZIP.
10. **FAQ** — insurance-focused ("Will my insurance cover this?", "What's my deductible?", "How long does drying take?").
11. **Sticky footer** — phone + text + form as persistent bottom bar on mobile.

## Hero composition

- **Imagery:** action shot — crew with drying equipment on-site, or a homeowner shaking hands with a technician. NOT flood water (that's the fear, don't lead).
- **Primary CTA:** phone number — massive, click-to-call.
- **Secondary CTA:** "Text a photo" form (photo upload + phone number).
- **Social proof inline:** star rating + review count + "24/7 emergency response."

## Trust stack ordering

1. **IICRC certification** (industry standard — must have)
2. **State licensing + insurance / bond numbers**
3. **BBB A+**
4. **Insurance carriers we work with** (Allstate, State Farm, Farmers, USAA logos)
5. **Google reviews aggregate**
6. **RIA (Restoration Industry Association) membership** if applicable
7. **Chamber of Commerce local trust**

## Form patterns

- **Photo upload is a big conversion lift.** Customer can send a photo of the damage without describing it. Requires phone + photo only.
- **2-step form:** Step 1 emergency Y/N — Step 2 contact info. Segments emergency from planned mitigation.
- **Phone-primary conversion** — mitigation is a call business. Forms are secondary.

## Sticky elements

- **Mobile bottom bar:** call, text, form — always visible.
- **Header phone number:** persistent on scroll.
- **Emergency banner:** "24/7 available NOW" with time-of-day dynamic swap (nice-to-have).

## Divergence from universal converting wireframe

Mitigation diverges from the universal home-services template even more than roofing does. The universal template assumes a planned purchase (research → compare → contact). Mitigation is a **crisis purchase** (find phone → dial → hope someone answers). The design must:

- Prioritize the phone over the form (opposite of most templates)
- Show a response-time commitment above the fold ("60-minute response")
- Minimize distractions (no long scrolling copy — crisis-mode users don't read)
- Show ONE emotion in imagery: relief. Not urgency, not fear — because they're already there emotionally.

## Mobile-first is critical

70%+ of mitigation traffic is mobile — someone standing in their basement holding their phone. Every design decision should be mobile-first. Desktop is secondary.
