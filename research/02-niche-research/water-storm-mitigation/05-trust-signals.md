# Water/Storm Mitigation — Trust Signals

**Rank from review evidence:** 58.7% of 4,488 reviews mention trust themes. Mitigation trust hinges specifically on speed to on-site + process transparency (educating during crisis).

## Top 5 trust signals

### 1. IICRC certification
The Institute of Inspection Cleaning and Restoration Certification. Table-stakes for legitimacy in this industry. Every winning mitigation site displays this prominently. Subtypes to highlight: **WRT (Water Restoration Technician), ASD (Applied Structural Drying), AMRT (Applied Microbial Remediation Technician)**.

### 2. Response-time commitment (with a number)
"60-minute response" is the industry-winning claim. Some claim "30 minutes" or "on-site within 90." The number must be published and testable, and ideally paired with a guarantee ("or your inspection is free").

### 3. Insurance-carrier logos + direct-billing language
"We work directly with your insurance carrier so you focus on getting your home back. You pay only your deductible." Displayed with major carrier logos (Allstate, State Farm, USAA, Farmers, etc.).

### 4. Owner's picture + owner's phone number
Restoration is a low-trust industry. The customer is usually calling in a crisis. Showing an owner's face + direct number ("Call {name} directly, day or night") beats the corporate "call our team" every time.

### 5. Photo/video documentation of past jobs
Before/after mitigation photos — pre-drying meter readings, drying equipment on-site, mold spots removed, final moisture readings. This proves the process is real and documented for insurance.

## Niche-specific certifications and badges

- **IICRC** (the whole certification tree: WRT, ASD, AMRT, FSRT, OCT)
- **RIA (Restoration Industry Association)** membership
- **State contractor / restoration license** (varies by state; Florida requires CGC, Tennessee requires HIB, etc.)
- **EPA Lead-Safe Certified** (for older homes; important for asbestos and lead-paint work)
- **BBB A+**
- **Home Advisor Screened & Approved** / **Angi Certified**

## Press / "as seen in" expectations

- Local news mentions after major storm events (hurricane, tornado, flood)
- Community involvement / disaster response volunteer work
- Servpro / Belfor logos are not press — but positioning against them as a local alternative is powerful

## Photo / video testimonial expectations

- Video walkthrough of a completed mitigation job, ~2 mins, homeowner narrates the crisis + resolution
- Photo of dried, cleaned space with moisture reading meter visible (proof of drying)
- Text testimonial from homeowner mentioning the response time and insurance win

## Anti-patterns (killed trust in review data)

- Chemical exposure not explained upfront ("They sprayed chemicals in the house and forgot to tell us we should not be in the house")
- No-shows with no communication
- Refusing small jobs ("just my garage") — makes the company look like they only chase big insurance payouts
- Slow follow-up after initial contact
- Any "franchise" branding in the hero — hurts the local-owner trust play
