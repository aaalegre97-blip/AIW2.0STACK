# Water/Storm Mitigation — Money Math

## Average ticket size (US)

- **Small water event (contained leak, minor drying):** $2,000 - $5,000
- **Medium water event (basement flood, single-floor):** $8,000 - $15,000
- **Large water event (multi-floor flood, sewage backup):** $15,000 - $40,000
- **Fire + water combined:** $25,000 - $60,000
- **Mold remediation only:** $2,500 - $10,000
- **Storm-driven full restoration (hurricane / tornado):** $15,000 - $75,000
- **Blended average per closed job:** ~$9,500 (skewed by high-value insurance-covered emergencies)

## Close rate math

- **Emergency inbound call at 2 AM:** 70-85% close rate. Customer needs someone NOW.
- **Referral from plumber/insurance adjuster:** 60-75% close
- **Paid ads (Google LSA):** 20-30% lead-to-inspection, 40-50% inspection-to-close
- **SEO / GBP inbound:** 40-60% inspection-to-close
- **Best channel:** Google LSA + GBP for emergency traffic

## Leads needed per month for a meaningful business

Assume Alan's mitigation client wants **12 emergency jobs/month** at $9,500 average = **$114,000/month gross**.

At **50% inspection-to-close** (typical for warm emergency inbound), that's ~24 inspections/month.

At **35% lead-to-inspection** (accounting for after-hours emergency calls that don't need mitigation after all), that's ~70 qualified leads/month.

At **10% CTR-to-lead** on a well-built emergency landing page, that's ~700 warm clicks/month — achievable with $3-4k/mo in Google LSA + GBP.

## The sentence Alan can say to a restoration prospect

> "If we get you 70 emergency leads a month at $4,000 ad + retainer spend, and your inspection-to-close is 50%, you'd close 12 emergency jobs a month at $9,500 average. That's $114,000 in new revenue. The system pays for itself in the first week of month two."

## Website ROI math for the offer

- Alan's package: $4,800 PIF setup + assume $1,500/mo retainer
- Client's payback on the $4,800: less than one closed job at any ticket size
- Client's annual gross new revenue if system holds: $1,368,000

## Seasonality caveat

- **Peak revenue:** Hurricane season (June-November for Gulf/Atlantic markets), winter freeze-thaw (December-February for northern markets), spring storms (March-May everywhere)
- **Trough:** Summer months in northern markets, winter in Gulf markets
- Restoration retainers must include GBP + review generation + educational content year-round so lead flow doesn't collapse in slower months
- **Off-season play:** target mold, radon, indoor air quality — these are year-round, non-emergency service lines
