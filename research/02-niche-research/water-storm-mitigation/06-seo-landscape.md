## Secondary / long-tail

- City-modified variants: `water damage {city}`, `flooded basement {city}`, `mold removal {city}`
- Insurance-specific: "does homeowners insurance cover water damage," "what does insurance cover for flood," "water damage claim process"
- Damage-type: "washing machine leak damage," "roof leak water damage," "AC condensate flood"
- Comparison: "IICRC vs uncertified restoration," "Servpro vs local restoration"

## Geographic targeting for the 3 sampled cities

### Tampa, FL
- HIGH competition (hurricane market — 100+ restoration companies within 25 miles)
- Servpro / Belfor / SteamMaster dominate PPC
- Blue-ocean angle: neighborhood + hurricane-name (e.g., "Hurricane {Name} damage cleanup Tampa Bay")
- Best months: June-November (hurricane season)

### Chattanooga, TN
- MEDIUM competition. ~50 restoration companies.
- Storm-water damage more common than flood.
- Long-tail: "water damage East Ridge," "flood cleanup Signal Mountain"

### Cheyenne, WY
- LOW competition. Fewer than 20 restoration companies within 50 miles.
- Best niche opportunity if Alan wants blue-ocean market
- Long-tail: "burst pipe Cheyenne," "frozen pipe damage" (winter market)

## Title / H1 patterns of top-ranking pages

- **Title:** `Water Damage Restoration in {City} | {Business Name} — 24/7 Emergency`
- **H1:** `{City}'s Emergency Water Damage Response` or `24/7 Restoration Services in {City}`
- Meta description always includes phone number, "24/7 emergency," and "IICRC certified"

## Schema markup common

- LocalBusiness / EmergencyService
- Service (per subservice: WaterDamageRestoration, MoldRemediation, FireDamageRestoration)
- Review + AggregateRating
- FAQPage on insurance-focused FAQs
- OpeningHoursSpecification (24/7 signal to Google)

## Content gap analysis

**Ranking pages all cover:**
- Services (water, fire, mold, sewage, storm)
- Response time claim
- Insurance-friendly language
- Service area

**Gaps to fill:**
1. "How much does water damage restoration cost with insurance?" — specific cost transparency, few good pages
2. "What happens if my adjuster says the damage isn't covered?" — insurance-battle content, high intent
3. "24-hour timeline: what to expect from a water damage restoration crew" — process transparency
4. City-by-neighborhood pages (Ybor City, Hyde Park, South Tampa, etc.)
5. "Do I have to move out during water damage restoration?" — common fear, poorly served
6. Seasonal content (hurricane prep, frozen pipe prep) — timed content assets

## Local SEO priorities

1. **GBP dominance** — mitigation is 90% local pack traffic. GBP posts, photos, Q&A must be active daily during storm season.
2. **Review velocity** — 5+ new Google reviews per month. Ask after every completed job.
3. **NAP consistency** across BBB, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Yelp, Nextdoor, Chamber
4. **After-storm GBP posts** — real photos of crews responding to named storms build seasonal authority
5. **Backlinks from insurance sites, real estate sites, and property management firms**
