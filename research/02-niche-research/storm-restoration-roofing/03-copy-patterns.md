# Storm-Restoration Roofing — Copy Patterns

## Top 10 hero headline patterns (from review-mined language and known niche sites)

1. **Insurance advocate framing:** "We fight your insurance company, so you don't have to."
2. **Storm-specific urgency:** "Roof damaged in the recent storm? Free inspection, no cost to you."
3. **Deductible-only:** "Your only out-of-pocket is your deductible. We handle the rest."
4. **Local pride + years:** "{City}'s roofing crew since {year}. {N} roofs replaced."
5. **Trust-first (not urgency):** "Honest inspections. If you don't need a new roof, we tell you."
6. **Speed promise:** "Damage inspection in 24 hours. Roof on your house in 7 days."
7. **Neighbor-referral tone:** "{N} of your neighbors trusted us this year."
8. **Warranty forward:** "Backed by a real {N}-year warranty we actually honor."
9. **No-pressure:** "No door knocking. No pressure. Just a free inspection and honest opinion."
10. **Insurance results:** "We got {N}% of denied claims approved last year."

## Top 10 CTA patterns

1. "Get my free inspection" (still the workhorse)
2. "Schedule my inspection" (more grown-up feel)
3. "Text {number} for a same-day quote" (speed-forward)
4. "See if my damage qualifies" (curiosity, less commitment)
5. "Get my roof approved by insurance" (outcome-specific)
6. "Book a 15-min call with the owner" (relationship)
7. "Free storm damage assessment" (event-triggered)
8. "Meet our project manager" (put a name/face)
9. "Get a video-inspection walkthrough" (differentiated)
10. "Talk to someone today, not tomorrow" (speed rule)

## Top 10 value props (extract from customer voice)

1. **Insurance advocacy** — "We handle the claim on your behalf."
2. **Honesty guarantee** — "If your roof is fine, we'll tell you and leave."
3. **Speed** — "Same-day inspection. On-site within 4 hours."
4. **Clean-up promise** — "Zero nails left in your driveway. Guaranteed."
5. **Warranty transparency** — "10-year workmanship, 30-year manufacturer, in writing."
6. **No-pressure sales** — "You'll never hear from us again if you say no."
7. **Local & permit-legit** — "Licensed, bonded, insured in {state}."
8. **Public adjuster network** — "We work with certified public adjusters."
9. **Communication cadence** — "You'll get a photo update every day of the install."
10. **Deductible-only pricing** — "You pay your deductible. Insurance covers the rest."

## Common ad hook structures (Facebook/Meta creative)

1. **Storm damage before/after** — hail damage close-up → new roof shot. Big proof.
2. **Testimonial video** — homeowner talks about insurance win. First 3 seconds: "I never thought insurance would pay for a full replacement."
3. **Founder-to-camera** — owner explains the storm-restoration process in 60s. Trust play.
4. **Neighborhood targeting** — "This week we replaced {N} roofs in {ZIP}. Here's how we can check yours." Local + urgency.
5. **Insurance denial reversal** — "Your claim was denied? Here's what to do next." Educational hook that captures a warm intent segment.
