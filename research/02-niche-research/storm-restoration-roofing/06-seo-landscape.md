## Secondary / long-tail clusters

- **City-modified variants:** "roofing companies {city}", "{city} storm damage roofer", "hail damage roof {city}" — hundreds of low-volume, low-competition long-tail. These are the workhorse keywords for local SEO.
- **Insurance-specific:** "will insurance cover roof replacement," "how to file a roof insurance claim," "what to do if roof claim denied" — great for blog / educational content.
- **Damage-type specific:** "wind damage roof," "hail damage shingles," "tree damage roof" — long-tail with clear commercial intent.
- **Comparison / brand:** "GAF vs Owens Corning roofing," "best shingles for hail" — high-intent research phase.

## Geographic targeting for the 3 sampled cities

### Dallas, TX
- GBP density is HIGH (200+ roofers within 25 miles per GMB)
- Competitive keyword battle — top spots owned by large national roofers (Berkshire, Empire, Ready)
- Blue-ocean angle: neighborhood-level SEO (e.g., "roofer in Frisco TX", "storm damage in Preston Hollow")
- Storm season (spring hail, tornado risk) drives massive lead volume — hail season is Feb-May

### Springfield, MO
- GBP density MEDIUM (~40-60 roofers)
- Less competition than Dallas, easier to rank
- Hail belt — good storm-restoration market
- Local SEO focused on Springfield + surrounding counties (Greene, Christian, Webster)

### Cheyenne, WY
- GBP density LOW (10-20 roofers) — biggest local SEO opportunity but tiny market
- High wind damage (Wyoming's second-most common storm damage category after hail)
- Long-tail: "wind damage roofer Cheyenne," "hail damage Laramie County"

## Title and H1 patterns of top-10 ranking pages (typical patterns)

- **Title:** `{City} Roofers | {Business Name} | Storm Damage Specialists`
- **H1:** `Storm-Damage Roofing in {City}` or `{City}'s Trusted Roofing Contractor`
- Common suffix in title: `| Free Inspection & Insurance Claim Help`
- Meta description: 130-155 chars, always includes phone number

## Schema markup commonly used

- **LocalBusiness** (schema.org/LocalBusiness) — required for GBP linkage
- **RoofingContractor** (specific subtype of LocalBusiness)
- **Service** for individual service pages (Roof Replacement, Roof Repair, Storm Damage)
- **Review** and **AggregateRating** on homepage — pulls star display in SERPs
- **FAQPage** on FAQ pages — pulls expanded results in SERP
- **BreadcrumbList** for internal navigation

## Content gap analysis (what ranking pages all cover, and what you can fill)

**They all cover:**
- Services offered (replacement, repair, gutters, siding as upsell)
- Service areas (cities list)
- Reviews / testimonials
- Photo gallery

**Common gaps to fill (with content pages):**
1. **"What happens if my insurance denies my roof claim?"** — high-intent long-tail, few good pages exist
2. **"How to spot hail damage on a shingle"** — educational, homeowner-focused
3. **"Timeline of a storm-damage roof replacement from inspection to install"** — process transparency, few pages cover this well
4. **City-neighborhood pages** — most sites have city pages but miss neighborhood-level (Preston Hollow, Frisco, Highland Park, etc.)
5. **Public adjuster vs insurance adjuster — what's the difference?** — a topic homeowners search when confused, poorly served

## Local SEO priorities (biggest ranking factor for this niche)

1. **Google Business Profile completeness** — full service list, photos updated weekly, Q&A active
2. **Review velocity** — 3-5 new Google reviews per month minimum. Solicit after every completed job.
3. **Citation consistency** — NAP (name/address/phone) identical across BBB, HomeAdvisor, Angi, Nextdoor, Chamber
4. **GBP posts** — weekly posts about recent jobs, storm alerts, tips. Signals active business.
5. **Backlinks from insurance and public adjuster sites** — natural, hard-earned, hugely valuable
