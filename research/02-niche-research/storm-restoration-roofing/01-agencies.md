## Known agencies that specialize in roofing marketing (US)

### Roofing Marketing Pros (roofingmarketingpros.com)
- **Approach:** Pay-for-performance (leads or booked appointments). Runs Meta + Google Ads. Big on ClickFunnels-style single-offer landing pages.
- **Pricing:** $2k-$5k/mo retainers plus paid ad spend. Some pay-per-lead offers at $75-$200/lead.
- **Sells:** Volume-of-leads promise, not brand. "50 exclusive leads per month" framing.
- **What they miss:** Retention. They win the lead but leave the roofer to close and keep the customer. No SEO or long-term brand assets.

### Hook Agency (hookagency.com)
- **Approach:** SEO-heavy, content marketing, focus on organic ranking for roofers. Publishes public case studies.
- **Pricing:** $1.5k-$4k/mo. Transparent packages on site.
- **Sells:** Long-term inbound. "Rank #1 in your city for 'roofer near me'."
- **What they miss:** Slow to show ROI (3-6 months for SEO to kick in). Roofers who need cash flow this month churn.

### Blue Corona (bluecorona.com)
- **Approach:** Full-service digital agency for home services broadly. Big-agency polish. National, not niche-specific.
- **Pricing:** $3k-$10k/mo. Enterprise-feel.
- **Sells:** Sophisticated dashboards, attribution. Feels safe.
- **What they miss:** Speed to first result. Onboarding is slow. Not a lot of niche-native language.

### Levergy (levergy.com)
- **Approach:** Roofing-specific coaching + agency hybrid. Big on community and mastermind.
- **Pricing:** Membership + services combined. $2k-$5k range.
- **Sells:** "Grow your roofing business" identity. Owner-to-owner language.
- **What they miss:** Delivery quality varies. Community focus dilutes agency execution.

### Contractor 20/20, ContractorDynamics, Rilla Voice — assorted others
Roofing marketing is a crowded market. Most operators are ex-roofers who now sell services. Table stakes: Google Ads, LSA, Meta creative, basic SEO, GBP management.

## Top 3 patterns winners share
1. **They speak the storm-restoration playbook fluently** — mention insurance, adjusters, public adjusters, deductible-only pricing on their site. Roofers know the site was written by someone who's been in this game.
2. **They show real dollar case studies** — "$X spent in ads, Y jobs closed, $Z revenue" not vanity metrics like impressions.
3. **They provide a hard deliverable early** — audit, GBP overhaul, or first campaign go-live in week 1-2. Nobody wants a 60-day "strategy phase."

## Top 3 patterns losers share
1. **Generic home-services positioning** — "we help contractors" doesn't beat "we help storm-restoration roofers."
2. **No public case studies** — all testimonials, no numbers.
3. **Complex tech stack** — HubSpot / Salesforce / Zapier flows for a 5-employee roofing shop. Roofers want text messages and phone calls, not dashboards.
