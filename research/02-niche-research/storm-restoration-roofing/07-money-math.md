# Storm-Restoration Roofing — Money Math

## Average ticket size (US, storm-restoration segment)

- **Roof replacement (full):** $12,000 - $25,000 (asphalt shingle, avg residential home)
- **Roof repair (storm damage):** $1,500 - $6,000 (partial replacement, patch)
- **Storm-damage inspection:** $0 (free, loss leader; converts to replacement quotes)
- **Insurance-covered replacement (the sweet spot):** homeowner pays $1,000-$2,500 deductible; roofer receives $15,000-$25,000 from insurance carrier
- **Blended average per closed job:** ~$14,500

## Close rate math (from industry benchmarks + review inspection language)

- **Cold canvassing (door knock):** 1-3% doors-to-inspection, ~30% inspection-to-signed contract → ~0.5% doors-to-close
- **Warm inbound (SEO / GBP / referral):** 30-50% inspection-to-signed contract
- **Paid ads (Meta / Google):** 5-10% lead-to-inspection, 25-35% inspection-to-close
- **Best channel:** GBP + organic reviews — 50%+ inspection-to-close because trust is pre-earned

## Leads needed per month for a meaningful business

Assume Alan's client — a mid-size storm-restoration roofer, wants to close **8 jobs/month** at $14,500 average = **$116,000/month gross**.

At a **30% inspection-to-close rate** (typical for warm inbound), that's ~27 inspections/month.

At a **50% lead-to-inspection rate** (also typical for warm inbound), that's ~54 qualified leads/month.

At a **10% CTR-to-lead rate** on a well-built landing page, that's ~540 warm clicks/month — very achievable with $2-3k/mo in Google Ads + GBP + basic SEO.

## Storm-restoration seasonality (money math caveat)

- **Peak revenue:** After spring storm season (March-June for Dallas, May-August for Springfield, June-August for Cheyenne)
- **Trough:** November-February
- Client retainers must be structured to survive the trough — offer year-round SEO + GBP + review generation so leads flow even in off-season
- Off-season is when the roofer should be running educational content and building neighborhood-level SEO
