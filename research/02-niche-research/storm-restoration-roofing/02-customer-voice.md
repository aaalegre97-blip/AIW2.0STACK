# Storm-Restoration Roofing — End Customer Voice

**Source:** 5,040 Google Maps reviews with text across Dallas (4,001), Springfield MO (794), Cheyenne WY (245). See `_summary.md` for raw stats.

## Top 5 fears (from negative reviews)

1. **Getting spammed / harassed after inquiry.** "They called me 2 to 3 times a day for a few weeks now. Keep telling them to leave me alone, they just keep calling." "Ring my doorbell despite my no-soliciting sign." Homeowners are exhausted by aggressive roofing canvassing.
2. **Ghost quote — company disappears after inspection.** "Came and looked at our roof over a month ago and never got the quote." "Was told I would get an estimate in 2 days. Never heard from them again."
3. **Being scammed / oversold.** "They tried to sell me valves again when I told the guy they were replaced last year." "Overpriced." "Extra things that didn't make sense."
4. **Getting a bad roof.** "Put on a faulty roof, when it was pouring water in my home, they refused to fix it. Threatened to put a lien on my property."
5. **Getting ignored after payment.** "When you pay in full don't expect them to honor your warranty." "They keep pushing you off."

## Top 5 trust signals customers volunteer

1. **Honesty over pushy sales** — "Honest Company — Greg came out and inspected our roof after this Spring's storms. As it turns out, our roof is fine! Love the honesty over a wheel-and-deal sales pitch!" This is the #1 differentiator vs the industry stereotype.
2. **Clean-up after work** — "Cleaned up after the work they did." "Left the yard cleaner than expected." Homeowners hate nail-in-the-tire risk.
3. **Communication cadence** — "Kept us informed as to what to expect." Real-time updates during the job.
4. **Professional appearance & vehicles** — "Look at the truck it looks brand on a company when you see a business vehicle violating driving/parking rules." Vehicle wraps and uniformed crews signal legit.
5. **Working with your insurance** — "The project manager was very attentive to my questions and concerns the whole way through the insurance process."

## Decision moments (when the customer says they made the call)

- **After a storm/hail event** — "Our roof sustained damage in the Dallas EF3 tornado." "Recent weather and hail." Time-sensitive.
- **After seeing damage during another repair** — "I had a leak in my roof." Reactive, not planned.
- **After a competitor tried to upsell/scam them** — "The insurance only approved a repair on my roof but Jorge and his team worked their magic and somehow got my roof fully replaced." Comparison shopping is common.
- **After a neighbor referral** — many reviews mention "my neighbor used them."

## Key takeaway for the copy
The end customer is exhausted by aggressive salesmanship. The differentiator is **honesty + speed + follow-through**. A roofer's website should read like a calm neighbor, not a hail-chasing storm chaser. The best roofers surface honesty and process transparency, not urgency.
