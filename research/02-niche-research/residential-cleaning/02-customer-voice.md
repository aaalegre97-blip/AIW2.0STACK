# Residential Cleaning — End Customer Voice

**Source:** 5,396 Google Maps reviews with text across Chattanooga (3,238), Cheyenne (2,158). Las Vegas cleaning scrape failed (Google throttling). Note: "house cleaning service" as a search term returned adjacent home services (moving, plumbing, tree removal, brick washing, insulation) mixed with pure cleaning companies. This is representative of how Google groups the vertical — reviews still surface valid cleaning-customer language.

## Top 5 fears (from negative reviews)

1. **No-show / broken appointment.** "I booked a Sunday appointment from 1:00 to 3:00 on a Saturday night. No one showed. We never received any communication."
2. **Untrustworthy cleaners in the home.** Customers repeatedly mention feeling watched-over, whether keys/property are safe.
3. **Bait-and-switch pricing.** "They quote you $69 they come to your house and then they want $300."
4. **Cleaner turnover / new person every visit.** Not surfaced directly in reviews but is a well-known LTV killer — repeat customers hate a new cleaner each week.
5. **Missed spots / superficial cleans.** "Each person's idea of cleaning is very different."

## Top 5 trust signals customers volunteer

1. **"Every month" / recurring language** — "They are at my house every month to make sure everything is working correctly." Loyalty is highly volunteered.
2. **Named cleaners** — "Logan is our tech and has been great to work with for the last several years." Customers loyal to specific cleaners. Retention play.
3. **On-time arrival** — "They always call me ahead of time before showing up." Communication cadence matters.
4. **Attention to detail / thoroughness** — "Left the yard cleaner than expected." Sub-item detail-orientation.
5. **Family-owned / small-business trust** — "Amazing family oriented business!" Preferred over corporate franchise feel.

## Decision moments

- **Life transition:** moving in/out, spring cleaning, holiday hosting, post-renovation cleanup, baby coming home.
- **Overwhelmed by dual-income scheduling** — most residential cleaning customers are dual-income households.
- **Post-airbnb turnover** — vacation rental owners, high volume small clean.
- **Post-Party / event cleanup**
- **Elderly parent/relative needing assistance** — increasingly common.

## Key takeaway
Cleaning customers care most about **reliability, consistency of cleaner, upfront pricing, and thoroughness**. The winning positioning is not "sparkling clean" (everyone claims that) — it's **"the same cleaner every visit, on time, no surprises on price."** Customer LTV explodes when the customer builds a relationship with a specific cleaner. Retention math dominates acquisition math.
