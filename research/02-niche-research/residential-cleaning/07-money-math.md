# Residential Cleaning — Money Math

## Average ticket size (US, residential cleaning)

- **Standard weekly clean (2 bed / 2 bath):** $110 - $180
- **Standard biweekly clean (average home):** $150 - $250
- **Standard monthly clean:** $200 - $300
- **Deep clean (one-time):** $250 - $600
- **Move-in / move-out clean:** $350 - $800
- **Post-construction / post-renovation:** $500 - $1,500
- **Airbnb turnover (small):** $60 - $120 per turnover
- **Airbnb turnover (large):** $150 - $300 per turnover

Per-job ticket is LOW compared to roofing/mitigation. **The LTV math is what makes cleaning viable.**

## LTV math (this is where cleaning wins)

A biweekly recurring customer at $200/clean:
- 26 cleans per year × $200 = **$5,200 annual revenue per customer**
- Average customer tenure: 18-24 months (industry data)
- **Customer LTV: $7,800 - $10,400**

Compare to roofing's $14,500 per job (one-time). Cleaning is a recurring-revenue machine.

## Close rate math

- **Instant online quote leads:** 25-40% quote-to-first-clean (very high because the quote is already there)
- **Phone-inquiry leads:** 40-60% quote-to-first-clean
- **Referral leads:** 60-80% quote-to-first-clean (highest)
- **Best channel:** GBP + organic reviews + referral programs

## Leads needed per month for a meaningful business

Assume Alan's cleaning client wants to add **20 new recurring customers per month**. Each becomes a $5,200/year customer over their 18-24 month lifespan.

At **35% quote-to-first-clean** (typical for warm inbound), that's ~57 qualified quote requests/month.

At **8% CTR-to-quote** on a well-built cleaning site, that's ~715 warm clicks/month — achievable with $2-3k/mo in Google Ads + GBP + basic SEO.
