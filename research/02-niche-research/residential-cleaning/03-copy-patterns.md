# Residential Cleaning — Copy Patterns

## Top 10 hero headlines

1. **Consistency-first:** "The same cleaner. Every visit. On time. No surprises."
2. **Recurring-plan sell:** "$X per week for a spotless home. Cancel anytime."
3. **Trust-first:** "Background-checked, bonded, insured cleaners in {city} since {year}."
4. **Time-value framing:** "Get your Saturdays back. Cleaners you can trust in your home."
5. **Move-in/move-out specific:** "Moving? Get every deposit back with a professional deep clean."
6. **Post-renovation:** "Just finished a renovation? Skip the dust — we deep clean it out."
7. **Airbnb/short-term:** "Reliable turnover cleaning for {city} vacation rentals. 5-star ready."
8. **Family-owned local:** "{City}'s family-owned cleaning service. Not a franchise."
9. **Instant-quote:** "Get an instant online quote in 30 seconds. Book with one click."
10. **Retention-forward:** "94% of our customers are still with us after 12 months."

## Top 10 value props

1. **Same cleaner every visit** (relational, retention)
2. **Background-checked, bonded, insured** (safety)
3. **Recurring plan discount** (recurring lock-in)
4. **Transparent pricing** (per-square-foot or per-bedroom, published)
5. **100% happiness guarantee** (redo if not satisfied)
6. **Cancel anytime** (no contract friction)
7. **Eco-friendly / green cleaning supplies** (subset of buyers care deeply)
8. **All supplies provided** (customer doesn't need to buy anything)
9. **Locally owned, not franchise** (local trust)
10. **Instant online booking** (friction removal)

## Common ad hook structures

1. **Time-value visual** — cleaner working while family enjoys weekend outside. "You worked all week. Take Saturday back."
2. **Before-and-after** — messy kitchen → sparkling kitchen, dramatic angle
3. **Owner-to-camera** — "I run this cleaning company. Here's why we use the same cleaner every visit." Trust play.
4. **Testimonial video** — recurring customer explains their loyalty (long-time relationship = strong signal)
5. **Cleaner introduction** — meet a real cleaner on the team, first name basis. Anti-anonymous-corporate feel.
