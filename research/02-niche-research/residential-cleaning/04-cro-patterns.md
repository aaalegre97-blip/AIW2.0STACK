# Residential Cleaning — CRO Patterns

## Section order (winning residential cleaning sites)

1. **Hero with instant quote widget** — the winning cleaning site puts an instant online quote (square footage / bedrooms / bathrooms → price) directly in the hero. This is a huge conversion lift.
2. **Trust bar** — bonded/insured, background-checked, family-owned, {N} years serving {city}.
3. **Recurring plans grid** — weekly / biweekly / monthly with prices and small "most popular" tag.
4. **How it works (3-step visual)** — "Book online → Cleaner arrives → We handle everything."
5. **Meet the cleaners** — actual photos of cleaning team members with first names. Anti-anonymous.
6. **Photo/video testimonials** — recurring customer stories.
7. **What we clean checklist** — full room-by-room checklist. Transparency about scope.
8. **Add-ons and one-time services** — deep clean, move-in/out, post-renovation, Airbnb.
9. **Reviews strip** — Google star aggregate + 4-6 testimonials.
10. **FAQ** — pricing questions, cancellation, tipping, supplies, security.
11. **Booking form / phone / text as persistent footer**.

## Hero composition

- **Imagery:** cleaner-in-action shot with a happy homeowner in the background. NOT stock-photo maids in aprons (customers spot immediately).
- **Primary CTA:** instant online quote widget (square footage input) — forms > phone in this niche because customers hate calling.
- **Secondary CTA:** "Book by phone" for older demographics.
- **Social proof inline:** "★★★★★ 4.9 from {N} reviews" and "Locally owned in {city} since {year}."

## Trust stack ordering

1. Bonded, insured (both — customers ask about theft coverage)
2. Background-checked cleaners
3. Google reviews aggregate + count
4. Angi / HomeAdvisor / Yelp badges
5. Years in business + local ownership
6. BBB accreditation
7. Named team members / cleaner photos

## Form patterns

- **Instant quote wins**. Square footage / bedrooms + email + ZIP → price shown immediately. 3-5x conversion lift over "call for quote."
- **Booking calendar embedded**. Customers pick their date without a phone call.
- **Phone secondary**. Some older customers still call — provide a number in the header but don't lead with it.

## Sticky elements

- **Mobile bottom bar:** "Book now" primary, "Call" secondary.
- **Chat widget** — cleaning has high inbound chat traffic (customers ask about supplies, pets, specific requests).

## Divergence from universal converting wireframe

Residential cleaning diverges from the universal home-services wireframe in two important ways:
1. **Form-first, not phone-first.** Cleaning customers prefer online booking. Every friction point on the way to booking (having to call, having to email) is a killer.
2. **Recurring-plan emphasis.** Universal home services template treats each service as standalone. Cleaning must lead with the recurring plan structure to unlock the LTV math.

The site is more like a subscription checkout flow than a lead-gen page.

## Mobile considerations

- 65% of cleaning traffic is mobile
- Instant quote must work well on mobile (touch-friendly bedroom/bathroom pickers)
- Booking calendar must be mobile-first
