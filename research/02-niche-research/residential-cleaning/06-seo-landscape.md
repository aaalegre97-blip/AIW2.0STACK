## Geographic targeting

### Las Vegas, NV (blocked in scrape but researchable)
- HIGH competition — massive cleaning market including Airbnb/vacation rentals
- Sub-niches: Strip-adjacent short-term rentals, retiree residents on the west side, Summerlin high-end residential
- Strong opportunity in vacation-rental cleaning specifically (thousands of Airbnb/VRBO units)

### Chattanooga, TN (4083 reviews scraped)
- MEDIUM competition — ~50 cleaning companies
- More residential recurring plans, less vacation rental
- Long-tail: "house cleaning North Chattanooga," "maid service Hixson"

### Cheyenne, WY (2806 reviews scraped)
- LOW competition — under 20 cleaning companies within 50 miles
- Small-town market, recurring plans mostly, minimal vacation rental
- Long-tail: "house cleaning Cheyenne," "move-out cleaning Laramie County"

## Title / H1 patterns

- **Title:** `House Cleaning in {City} | {Business Name} — Same Cleaner Every Visit`
- **H1:** `{City}'s Trusted Recurring House Cleaning Service`
- Meta: 130-155 chars with "bonded, insured, background-checked" trust signals

## Schema markup common

- **LocalBusiness / HomeAndConstructionBusiness**
- **Service** for individual services (Recurring Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Move-Out Cleaning)
- **Review + AggregateRating**
- **FAQPage**
- **OpeningHoursSpecification**
- **Product / Offer** for recurring plans with price

## Content gap analysis

**Ranking pages all cover:**
- Services list (recurring, deep clean, move-in/out)
- Pricing (some)
- Reviews
- Service area

**Gaps to fill:**
1. "Cleaning cost calculator" — most sites lack a transparent square-foot pricing tool
2. "What to expect on your first cleaning" — process transparency
3. "Should I tip my cleaner? How much?" — common Google search, poorly answered
4. Neighborhood pages (Downtown, Summerlin, Henderson, etc. in Vegas; North Chatt, Hixson in Chattanooga)
5. Sub-niche pages: Airbnb cleaning, post-construction, elderly-care cleaning, small business cleaning
6. Cleaning frequency comparison: "Weekly vs biweekly cleaning — what saves you more"

## Local SEO priorities

1. **GBP dominance** — cleaning is 80%+ local pack traffic
2. **Review velocity** — customers happy to leave reviews if asked (higher than roofing/mitigation). 8+ per month sustainable.
3. **Photo posts on GBP** — regular before/after posts
4. **Response to reviews** — cleaning customers respond well to acknowledgment (positive) and remediation (negative). Improves conversion.
5. **Neighborhood group presence** — Facebook neighborhood groups, Nextdoor, Buy Nothing groups drive substantial referral traffic
