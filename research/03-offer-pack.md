## Stage of business

**Offer shape:**
- **Design homepage upfront, free.** No deposit.
- **On approval (client loves the design):** $1,000 deposit unlocks the full build.
- **Balance:** $3,800 across 60 days (Net-30 x 2 payments), OR pay in full upon go-live for a small discount.
- **Ad-management retainer (optional add-on):** $1,500/mo for Google Ads + Meta management, month-to-month, cancel anytime.

**Cap this offer at 3 clients.** After 3 case studies are in the bank, prices move to full $4,800 PIF (no free upfront) and retainers become the norm, not the add-on.

## Offer mechanics

- **Risk reversal:** Free homepage design upfront. Zero cost to the prospect until they approve the design. If they walk, they keep the design mockup for free.
- **Price anchor (3-tier package):**
  1. **Rebuild** ($4,800 PIF): homepage rebuild + Google Business Profile overhaul + 3 service pages
  2. **Rebuild + Launch** ($4,800 + $1,500/mo): everything in Rebuild plus Meta ads campaign build and month-1 management
  3. **Full System** ($4,800 + $3,000/mo): Rebuild + Meta ads + Google Ads (LSA + Search) + review automation + monthly reporting
- **Prospects buy the middle 8 times out of 10.** Anchor the Full System to make Rebuild + Launch feel like the smart pick.
- **Payment terms:** For the first 3 free-upfront clients: $1,000 on approval + Net-30 x 2. After the first 3: 50% deposit, 50% on go-live.
- **Speed guarantee:** Homepage design mockup delivered within 5 business days. Full build live within 21 days of deposit. Miss either deadline and the next month of retainer is free.
